# Flowbase Product Brief

## Product

Flowbase is an AI-powered productivity workspace that unifies Notion-style notes, Miro-style whiteboards, kanban boards, calendar, tasks, AI assistance, AI template building, and real-time collaboration.

## Landing page goals

- Explain the product clearly and convert visitors into sign-ups.
- Present Flowbase as a polished, enterprise-level AI SaaS product.
- Use React, Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, ShadCN-compatible components, and Lucide icons.
- Keep interfaces reusable, responsive, accessible, and scalable.

## Required landing content

- Sticky navigation with logo, anchor links, login, and sign-up CTA.
- Hero: AI productivity value proposition, Get Started and Watch Demo CTAs, trust signals, and dashboard visual.
- Feature highlights: smart dashboard, AI assistant, calendar and reminders, kanban boards, notes, whiteboards, AI template builder, live collaboration, and settings or custom categories.
- How it works: organize the workspace, let AI plan and create, collaborate and track progress.
- Product showcase: dashboard, notes, kanban, whiteboard, and AI assistant visuals.
- AI capabilities: task creation, reminders, note refinement, diagram generation, template or mini-app creation, and productivity insights.
- Collaboration: shared boards, active user presence, task comments, Liveblocks-powered collaboration, and team workspaces.
- Use cases: founders, students, teams, creators, project managers, and personal productivity.
- Pricing: Free, Pro, and Team plans.
- Three professional testimonials, FAQ, final conversion CTA, and product/resources/legal/social footer.

## Design direction

- Cozy, clean, modern, and professional SaaS design.
- Premium polish through soft gradients, restrained glass-like surfaces, rounded corners, subtle tinted shadows, and smooth hover feedback.
- Use one consistent accent color and maintain a clear light/dark theme strategy.
- Prefer authentic product visuals over CSS-built fake screenshots.
- Desktop, tablet, and mobile must be intentionally responsive.
